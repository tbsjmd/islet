Questions

Does the Assistant cost anything?

Instant commands are free and offline. With a Claude or Google sign-in it uses the plan or free quota you already have. With an API key, your provider bills you directly. A multi-step task uses one request per step.

Can it send email or delete things on its own?

No. When it operates an app, any step that sends, posts, buys, deletes or can't be undone stops and waits for your Do it. It asks once before it takes over the mouse and keyboard, never types into password fields, and can't run commands or scripts.

How do I stop it mid-task?

Press Esc, press ⌃⌥ again, or click stop in the island. It stops at once.

Does ⌃⌥ get in the way of my shortcuts?

It starts listening only after you hold those two keys alone for about a third of a second. Press another key or click within that moment and it cancels, so quick shortcuts and modifier-drags keep working.

Does it work on a Mac without a notch?

Yes. The island floats as a capsule at the top center of the screen, and can show on every display at once.

Is it open source?

Yes, under the GNU GPL v3 or later. The whole app is on GitHub. Islet builds on Vorssaint Utils, Textream, Parabolic, Atticus and Plip.
